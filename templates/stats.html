How Wins and Losses Work

You get a loss only when you finish a complete game and Marta beats you. That's it.

You lose when:

  • Someone reaches 300 points (or gets a 300+ point lead), AND Marta's score is higher than yours at that moment
  • OR you attempt Blind Nil and fail (instant loss)

What DOESN'T count as a loss:

  • Quitting or abandoning a game mid-play
  • Losing individual hands during a game
  • Going negative in score
  • Failing your bids
The key point: Only completed games count toward wins and losses. If you start a game but don't finish it, that's tracked separately as "abandoned" - it doesn't count as either a win or a loss in your competitive stats.
